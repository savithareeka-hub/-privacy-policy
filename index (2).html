Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Wayn Tech Cards ("we", "us") collects, uses and protects personal information when we run our printing business and send invoices and order updates to customers, including through WhatsApp.

Information we collect

  • Customer name, business name, phone / WhatsApp number, email and address.
  • GSTIN and billing details provided for invoicing.
  • Order, quotation and invoice details (items, quantities, amounts, payment status).

How we use information

  • To prepare quotations, orders and invoices and to deliver printed products.
  • To send invoices (PDF), payment reminders and order updates to customers by WhatsApp, phone or email.
  • To maintain business and accounting records as required by law.

WhatsApp messaging

We use the WhatsApp Business Platform (provided by Meta) to send messages and documents to customers who have done business with us or contacted us. Messages sent through WhatsApp are also subject to WhatsApp's own terms and privacy policy. You can ask us at any time to stop sending you messages.

Sharing of information

We do not sell personal information. We share it only with service providers needed to run our business (for example, cloud hosting and Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform for message delivery), or where required by law.

Data security and retention

We store data in access-controlled systems and keep it only as long as needed for business, tax and legal purposes.
